Section 1 — Introduction

Existing Operational Challenges

The current onboarding process is manual, fragmented, and time-consuming — creating bottlenecks that impact client experience and team productivity.

Time Consumption

  • 4–5+ hours per client onboarding
  • Multiple disconnected systems to manage
  • Manual reminders & follow-ups
  • Manual status tracking across tools

Major Issues

  • Delayed onboarding completion
  • Human errors in data handling
  • No central visibility of progress
  • Missing documents & poor compliance
  • Poor scalability for growth
